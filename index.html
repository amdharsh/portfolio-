BioDetect AI

A biomarker-based disease risk platform. You upload a blood panel report and gene expression data, and it returns a risk prediction across four diseases: ovarian cancer, type 2 diabetes, Alzheimer's and cardiovascular disease. Results are saved as records in the browser.

A carbon footprint calculator. You enter your monthly electricity use, daily travel distance and fuel use, and it produces an emission report and estimates how many carbon credits you would need to offset it.
